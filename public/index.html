Good to know

FAQ

Do I need any experience?

Nope. Most of our riders are first-timers. Your instructor walks you through everything and stays with you the whole session.

Is the $350 per person?

$350 covers the full 2-hour private experience for your group. Add everyone's name at checkout so we can prepare gear.

What should I bring?

Swimwear, sunscreen, a towel and good vibes. We provide boards, life vests, water and helmets for e-foil.

What if the weather is bad?

If conditions aren't safe, we'll reschedule you for free or give you a full refund.

Can I reschedule?

Yes, free of charge up to 24 hours before your session.
