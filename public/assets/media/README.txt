Put your videos here (MP4, H.264, ideally under ~8 MB each for fast loading):

  hero.mp4          full-screen background video at the top (landscape, 10-20s loop, no audio needed)
  hero-poster.jpg   still frame shown while the video loads (also used for link previews)
  wakesurf.mp4      wake surfing card video
  wakesurf-poster.jpg
  efoil.mp4         e-foil / e-surf card video
  efoil-poster.jpg

Instagram gallery: put photos/videos in the instagram/ folder, then run
  node scripts/build-gallery.js
File names can be changed in public/config.js.
